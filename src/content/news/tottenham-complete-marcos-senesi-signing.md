Tottenham Hotspur have confirmed the signing of defender Marcos Senesi on a free transfer, ending his spell at Bournemouth and adding another experienced centre-back option to Spurs' backline. The Argentine becomes the latest arrival in what has already been a busy summer of recruitment in north London.

Senesi established himself as a reliable and composed defender during his time on the south coast, becoming a regular fixture in Bournemouth's back line and earning a reputation as a calm ball-playing centre-back comfortable stepping into midfield. His contract expiry allowed Spurs to negotiate the move without a transfer fee, making it a shrewd piece of business for a club looking to strengthen defensively.

The move continues Tottenham's efforts to build greater depth and competition throughout the squad after last season, with the club's recruitment team having identified experienced Premier League defenders as a priority. Senesi's familiarity with the demands of English football is expected to help him settle quickly into his new surroundings.

Bournemouth, for their part, will now turn to replacing a player who was a key contributor to their recent Premier League seasons, with the Cherries' recruitment staff already working on options to fill the gap left by his departure.
