Tottenham Hotspur have confirmed the signing of centre-back Marcos Senesi on a free transfer, bringing an end to his four-year stint at Bournemouth. The Argentine defender arrives at N17 without a fee changing hands after his contract on the south coast expired, giving Spurs a experienced addition to their backline ahead of the new campaign.

Senesi established himself as one of the Premier League's more reliable defenders during his time at Bournemouth, known for his composure in possession and willingness to step into midfield when required. His departure leaves a gap at the Vitality Stadium that Andoni Iraola's successor will need to address, while Tottenham gain a player who already has a strong working knowledge of the division's demands, meaning minimal adaptation time compared with a signing arriving from abroad.

For Spurs, the move fits a pattern of recruiting proven Premier League performers on free or reduced fees rather than committing to expensive transfer deals, a strategy the club has leaned on in recent windows as it balances squad investment with financial sustainability. Senesi's versatility, including his ability to play across a back three or in a conventional pairing, gives the incoming coaching staff options as they shape their defensive setup for the season ahead.

With the transfer completed, attention will turn to how Senesi is integrated into pre-season training and whether further defensive reinforcements follow before the window closes. Bournemouth, meanwhile, will assess their own recruitment needs having lost a key figure from a defence that helped the club challenge for European qualification. The free transfer represents a low-risk, potentially high-reward piece of business for Tottenham as they look to strengthen a squad aiming to build on recent progress.
