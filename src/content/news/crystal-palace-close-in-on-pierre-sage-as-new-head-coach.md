Crystal Palace are on the verge of naming Pierre Sage as their new head coach, with the club understood to be finalising terms to prise him away from Ligue 1 side Lens. The appointment would fill the vacancy left at Selhurst Park following Oliver Glasner's departure at the end of last season, and it marks Palace's first major move of the summer as they plan for the campaign ahead.

Sage has built a solid reputation in France, having steered Lens through a demanding period in a competitive Ligue 1 environment. His work there has been enough to put him on the radar of a Premier League club looking for a coach capable of organising a squad quickly and settling into a new league without a lengthy adjustment period. Talks between the two parties are said to be at an advanced stage, with an announcement expected once the final details are agreed.

For Palace, the timing reflects a desire to have a permanent appointment in place well before the new season gets under way, giving the incoming head coach time to assess the existing squad and identify where reinforcements are needed. The club will also be mindful of maintaining the defensive stability and cup pedigree built up in recent years under previous management.

Should the deal be completed, Sage would become one of several fresh faces occupying Premier League technical areas this summer, part of a wider wave of managerial change sweeping through the division. Palace fans will now be watching closely for confirmation, along with any early signals about transfer targets the incoming coach may want to pursue before deadline day.
