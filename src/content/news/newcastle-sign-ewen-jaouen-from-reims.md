Newcastle United have completed the signing of Ewen Jaouen after the French defender passed a medical ahead of his move from Stade de Reims. The deal brings a player from outside the traditional recruitment pipeline of Europe's top leagues, with Newcastle instead identifying a target in the French second tier who they believe has the physical and technical attributes to make the step up.

Jaouen has been earmarked as a long-term prospect at St James' Park, arriving with a reputation built on standout performances in Ligue 2 that caught the attention of Newcastle's recruitment staff. His size and physical presence have been highlighted as a key part of his appeal, marking him out as a different profile to many of the club's recent signings.

The move reflects Newcastle's broader transfer strategy of scouting further afield for value, rather than restricting their search to established top-flight leagues. Bringing in a player from the second tier of French football carries an element of risk, but also the potential for significant reward if he adapts quickly to Premier League standards.

With the transfer now finalised, attention turns to how Jaouen will be integrated into Newcastle's squad during pre-season. The club will be keen to give him time to adjust to a new country, a new league, and a considerably faster style of football before competitive fixtures begin.
