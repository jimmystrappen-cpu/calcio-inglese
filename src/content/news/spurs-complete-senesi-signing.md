Tottenham have confirmed the signing of defender Marcos Senesi on a free transfer, bringing the Argentine to north London following the expiry of his contract at Bournemouth. The move gives Spurs fresh options at the back as they continue to reshape their squad ahead of the new Premier League season.

Senesi built a strong reputation during his time on the south coast, established as a reliable and composed presence in Bournemouth's defence across several seasons in the top flight. His departure on a free transfer represents a notable loss for the Cherries, even as it hands Tottenham a proven Premier League operator without any transfer fee attached.

For Spurs, the addition addresses a need for defensive depth and experience, with Senesi arriving already accustomed to the physical and tactical demands of English football. Unlike a new arrival from an unfamiliar league, he requires no adjustment period to the intensity of the Premier League, which could make him available for selection early in pre-season.

The move continues a busy summer of squad rebuilding for Tottenham, who have been active in reshaping their options across multiple positions. Bournemouth, meanwhile, will now need to assess how to fill the gap left by Senesi's exit as they too prepare for the campaign ahead.
