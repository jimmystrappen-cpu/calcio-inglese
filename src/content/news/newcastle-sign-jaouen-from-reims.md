Newcastle United are set to complete the signing of Ewen Jaouen from Stade de Reims, with the defender having already undergone a medical ahead of a permanent move to St James' Park. The deal takes Newcastle into France's second tier for reinforcements, a market Eddie Howe's recruitment team has monitored closely as they look for value additions alongside more established Premier League and continental targets.

Jaouen's imposing physical profile has been a talking point among those tracking the move, with his size and presence expected to give Newcastle's backline an additional aerial and defensive option. Details of the transfer fee have not been made public, but the club's willingness to complete the medical stage suggests personal terms and the structure of the deal were already largely agreed before the move was made known.

The signing continues a recruitment approach at Newcastle that has increasingly looked beyond the traditional big five European leagues for undervalued talent, mirroring similar bets the club has placed in past windows. Bringing in a player from Ligue 2 rather than a higher-profile league also reflects the financial planning Newcastle must navigate under Premier League profit and sustainability rules, with the club balancing ambition against regulatory constraints.

Should the move be finalised, Jaouen would join a Newcastle squad preparing for the upcoming season under continued scrutiny over their transfer strategy following a summer already shaped by other incoming and outgoing business. The club's recruitment department has stressed a long-term view in recent windows, and Jaouen's arrival would fit that pattern of identifying developing talent early rather than competing at the top of the market for the most sought-after names.
