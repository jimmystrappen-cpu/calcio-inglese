Newcastle United have entered talks with Osasuna over a move for Spain winger Victor Munoz, adding another name to a summer rebuild that has already reshaped Eddie Howe's attacking options. The Magpies are understood to see Munoz as a versatile addition capable of playing across the front line.

The interest comes as Newcastle continue to reinvest funds freed up by this summer's departures, with the club keen to add depth in wide areas after a congested run of fixtures last season stretched their squad thin. Munoz's ability to operate on either flank is seen as a key part of his appeal, offering Howe tactical flexibility he has lacked in previous campaigns.

Osasuna have historically driven a hard bargain in negotiations for their most promising players, and any deal is likely to take shape over the coming weeks rather than be resolved quickly. Newcastle's recruitment team has moved decisively in several other deals this window, suggesting a willingness to meet a fair valuation rather than let talks drag into the final days before the deadline.

Should the move go through, Munoz would become the latest in a string of signings aimed at freshening up a Newcastle squad chasing another push for European football in 2026/27.
