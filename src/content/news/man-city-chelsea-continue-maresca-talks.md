Manchester City and Chelsea are continuing negotiations over Enzo Maresca, with City pushing to appoint the Italian as they look to reshape their coaching staff after a period of transition. Talks between the two clubs are understood to be ongoing, with a compensation package for Maresca's departure from Stamford Bridge still to be agreed.

Maresca has built a reputation as one of the more sought-after young coaches in the Premier League, and City's interest reflects a broader shift in how the club is approaching its next phase. Chelsea, for their part, are said to be reluctant to let their manager leave without a fee that reflects his standing, adding a layer of complexity to talks that had initially been expected to move quickly.

Neither club has commented publicly on the substance of the discussions, though the scale of interest from City is understood to have been made clear through direct contact between the two boardrooms. Any deal would represent one of the most significant managerial moves of the summer, given Maresca's profile and City's stature.

With pre-season commitments approaching, there is added incentive for both parties to reach a resolution soon, even if the final terms of any compensation are still being worked through.
