Crystal Palace are closing in on the appointment of Pierre Sage as their new head coach, with the Frenchman set to leave French side Lens to take charge at Selhurst Park. The deal is understood to be at an advanced stage, with both clubs working through the final details before any formal confirmation.

Sage has earned strong reviews in Ligue 1 for his work at Lens, guiding the club to respectable league finishes while building a reputation for organised, attacking football on a modest budget. That profile appears to have appealed to Palace's recruitment team as they searched for a coach capable of maintaining the club's competitive standing in the Premier League.

The appointment would represent Sage's first job in English football and a significant step up in profile, with Palace having established themselves as a stable top-flight club in recent seasons. Getting the appointment right will matter to a fanbase that has grown used to steady progress under the club's recent managerial regimes.

Further details, including the length of contract and Sage's backroom staff, are expected to be confirmed once the deal is finalised, with Palace keen to have their new head coach in place well before the Premier League season resumes.
