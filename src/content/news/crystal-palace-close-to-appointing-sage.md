Crystal Palace are closing in on the appointment of Pierre Sage as their new head coach, with the Lens manager the leading candidate to take charge at Selhurst Park. The move would end the club's search for a successor following Oliver Glasner's departure, a process that has been closely watched given Palace's recent progress under their outgoing manager.

Sage built his reputation in France by steering Lens to competitive league finishes with a style built on organisation and control, qualities that appear to have appealed to Palace's hierarchy as they look to maintain the stability the club enjoyed in recent seasons. Taking over a Premier League squad represents a significant step up for the Frenchman, who would be tasked with continuing the progress Palace have made while also adding his own imprint on how the team plays.

The timing of the appointment matters for Palace, who will want a new head coach in place well before the season begins in order to oversee pre-season preparations and have input on any remaining transfer business. A managerial change of this kind typically brings adjustments to tactical approach and squad selection, and Palace's recruitment staff will be working closely with Sage, or his representatives, to align incoming and outgoing transfer plans with his preferred style of play.

Palace fans will be watching closely to see whether Sage can replicate the momentum built by his predecessor, particularly given the club's ambitions to establish themselves as a consistent top-half presence. Formal confirmation is expected once the final details of the deal, including compensation to Lens, are settled, with the club likely to move quickly given the proximity of the new campaign.
