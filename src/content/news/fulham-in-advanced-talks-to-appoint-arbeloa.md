Fulham are in advanced discussions to appoint Alvaro Arbeloa as their next manager, with the former Real Madrid defender lined up to replace Marco Silva at Craven Cottage. Silva's exit brings to an end a lengthy spell in charge of the Cottagers, and the club are now moving quickly to identify his successor before pre-season preparations get fully under way.

Arbeloa's move to Fulham is understood to be part of a broader chain of managerial changes this summer, with Silva expected to head to Benfica and Jose Mourinho linked with a return to Real Madrid around the same period. A single well-connected agent has reportedly played a role in coordinating several of these switches, illustrating how interlinked the market for elite coaches has become heading into the new season.

For Fulham, the appointment of Arbeloa would represent a shift toward a coach with limited senior managerial experience in England but a strong pedigree from his playing career and subsequent work in Spanish football. The club's hierarchy will be banking on a smooth transition, given the compressed timeline before competitive fixtures resume, and will want early clarity on his coaching staff and pre-season plans.

Silva leaves Fulham with the club having established itself as a stable Premier League presence during his tenure. Attention will now turn to how quickly Arbeloa can settle in, assess the existing squad, and make his mark before the opening weeks of the new campaign.
