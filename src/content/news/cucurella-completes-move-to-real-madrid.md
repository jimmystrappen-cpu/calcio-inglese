Marc Cucurella has left Chelsea for Real Madrid, one of the more surprising pieces of business in a Premier League summer that has already produced several high-profile departures. The left-back had become a regular fixture under Enzo Maresca since arriving in west London, making the switch to Spain a notable loss for Chelsea's defensive options.

For Real Madrid, the deal adds a versatile, combative full-back to a squad being reshaped after a summer dominated by the 2026 World Cup, where Spain beat Argentina to lift the trophy. Cucurella's ability to play across the back line and press aggressively fits the profile Madrid have targeted as they look to close the gap on Barcelona domestically and in Europe.

Chelsea's business elsewhere this summer suggests the club is comfortable trading experienced squad members for transfer fees while continuing to lean on the younger core built up over the past two seasons. Maresca will now need to assess his defensive options before the campaign begins, with time running short before the opening weekend.

The transfer underlines how fluid the market has become between the Premier League and La Liga's biggest clubs this summer, with fees and wages in both leagues continuing to climb.
