Newcastle United have completed the signing of Ewen Jaouen from French second-tier side Stade de Reims, with the defender having passed a medical before putting pen to paper on Tyneside. The move brings a highly-rated young prospect to St James' Park as Eddie Howe's side continues to reshape its squad for the season ahead.

Jaouen has drawn attention in France for his imposing physical presence and assured performances at the back, qualities that have led some observers to already tag him as one of the more promising defensive talents to emerge from Ligue 2 in recent seasons. Newcastle's recruitment department, known for identifying value in less heavily scouted leagues, moved decisively to secure the deal.

The signing reflects Newcastle's broader transfer strategy of blending experienced Premier League operators with younger players carrying significant long-term potential, giving the club room to develop talent within its own structures. Jaouen will now join up with his new teammates as pre-season training intensifies.

Further details on the transfer fee and contract length are expected to be confirmed by the club in the coming days, with Newcastle continuing to be active in the market as they look to strengthen multiple areas of the squad before the window closes.
